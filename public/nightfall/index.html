Nightfall

The moderator has left the table.

A cinematic narrator for Werewolf. Nightfall runs the night, calls the roles, and times the debate with voice and sound — so everyone gets to play.

iPhone · iOS 18.6+ · Free to play

Werewolf needs a moderator — and that person never gets to play. Nightfall takes the chair. It narrates every phase, wakes the wolves in the dark, and reveals the fallen at dawn. You just sit down and play.

What's inside

Built to run the night

🎙️

Voice narration

Full voice-acted scripts guide every phase — no one has to read rules aloud or remember the order of operations.

🌑

Automated phases

Night falls, roles wake in sequence, morning breaks. Nightfall tracks the state so the table never loses the thread.

🃏

Guided role reveal

Pass the phone at setup for a private, one-at-a-time reveal. Everyone learns their role in secret.

⏱️

Discussion timers

Time the debate with a lock-screen bell, so the day phase ends on cue even when your screen is dark.

🔊

Cinematic sound

Ambient night beds, howls, tolling bells and stingers. Pair a Bluetooth speaker and the room goes quiet.

📳

Haptic feedback

Subtle taps confirm every secret action on the pass-and-play device — feel the game without looking.
