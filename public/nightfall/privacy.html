Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026

The short version

Nightfall collects nothing about you. There is no account, no tracking, and no analytics. Your settings stay on your device.

Who we are

Nightfall is an iOS app published by flux & form ("we," "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to the Nightfall app and this website.

Information we collect

None. Nightfall does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not know who you are.

The app stores your preferences — audio and haptic toggles, your chosen narrator voice, whether you've completed onboarding, and which expansions you own — in standard iOS on-device storage (UserDefaults). This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us.

No tracking or analytics

Nightfall contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking technologies. We do not use cookies, device identifiers, or any service that profiles you across apps or websites. As declared in the app's App Store privacy details, no data is collected and no data is used to track you.

Purchases

Expansions such as the Full Moon Edition are sold as in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Your payment is processed by Apple — we never see or receive your payment details. The app only learns whether a purchase entitlement is active on your device.

Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Nightfall schedules local notifications on your device to sound the discussion-timer bell when your screen is locked. These are generated on your device — nothing is sent to or through our servers, and we operate no servers that receive your data.

Children's privacy

Nightfall does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is intended for a general audience in line with its App Store age rating.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Because the app collects nothing today, any future change that involved data collection would be described here before it took effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at support@fluxandform.io.
