Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

VibeDeck is a real-time party app: a host controls the music through Apple Music, and guests join a party room to add tracks and vote on what plays next. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what we do with it. We keep it minimal on purpose — VibeDeck works without knowing who you are.

01Who We Are

VibeDeck is operated by Flux and Form ("we," "us," or "our"). If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@fluxandform.io.

02Information We Collect

We collect only what's needed to run a party in real time:

  • A nickname you choose. When you join a party as a guest, the name you enter is shown to others in the room. Use whatever you like — it doesn't have to be your real name.
  • Party activity. The tracks you add, the votes you cast, and your in-app "vibe" balance are stored so the queue and scores stay in sync for everyone in the room.
  • A device identifier. The host app generates a random identifier stored on the host's own device to remember it across sessions. Guests are issued a random reconnect token (stored securely on their device) so they can rejoin the same party if they drop off. Neither identifier is tied to your name, email, or a personal account.
  • Basic technical logs. Like most online services, our servers record standard connection information to keep the service running, secure, and debuggable.

We do not ask for or collect your email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or payment information. VibeDeck has no user accounts for guests and no in-app purchases.

03Apple Music

Music playback and catalog search are powered by Apple Music (MusicKit). A host connects their own Apple Music account through Apple's native authorization — VibeDeck never sees or stores your Apple ID, password, or Apple Music credentials. Your use of Apple Music through the app is also governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

04How We Use Your Information

We use the limited data above only to:

  • Run live party rooms and keep the queue, votes, and scores in sync.
  • Let guests rejoin a party they were already part of.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • Diagnose and fix technical problems.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to build advertising profiles. VibeDeck shows no ads.

05How Long We Keep It

Party data is short-lived. When a party ends, its room, queue, and votes are cleared. Guest reconnect tokens expire automatically after a short period (currently 14 days) if unused. We keep data only as long as it's needed to provide the service.

06Service Providers

We use trusted third-party infrastructure to operate VibeDeck — for example, cloud hosting and database providers that store party data on our behalf. These providers process data only to run the service and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

07Children

VibeDeck is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

08Your Choices

Because guests don't create accounts, VibeDeck holds very little that identifies you. You can leave a party at any time, and party data is cleared when the party ends. For any request regarding data associated with your device, email us at support@fluxandform.io.

09Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

10Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy? Reach us at support@fluxandform.io.
